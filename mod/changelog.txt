Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-07-25
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed four triggers that could never fire on the vanilla base game: Power Armor Mk2
      and Personal Roboport Mk2 used Space Age technology names, and rocket control units
      no longer exist in 2.0.
  Changes:
    - Phase 10 now tracks Battery Mk2 and Energy Shield Mk2 instead of the Portable Fusion
      Reactor, which is Space Age content and not available in the base game.
    - Phase 14 tracks the three real rocket components (processing units, low density
      structures, rocket fuel) at the ~715 each a Prod-3 silo needs, replacing the rocket
      control unit task and the old under-counted thresholds.
