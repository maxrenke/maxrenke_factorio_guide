Factorio Vanilla Rocket Launch Guide

Your complete reference for a first playthrough - from crash landing to rocket launch. Covers all 15 phases of progression on the Space Age patch (v2.x), vanilla only. No DLC content.

Phase 01
First Steps - Manual Phase
Crash landing to first automated production
In Progress
▼
💡
You just crash-landed with nothing. Before any automation exists, you're the factory - mining, crafting, and hauling everything by hand. This phase builds the raw material stockpile you need to bootstrap electricity and research.
🏁
Phase Goal: 8-10 iron furnaces, 4-6 copper furnaces, coal self-fueling, enough materials to build power and a lab.
Checklist
Press Alt immediately - this shows resource overlays on all machines so you can see what items are inside and what's being produced.
Loot your crashed ship with Ctrl + LClick - grab all iron plates and ammo from inside. Use Alt to see what's in there.
Find coal rocks on the map (M). These look like darker rocks and yield 20-40 coal + stone each. Mine every coal rock you find early on.
Use Alt+D (construction planner) to scan an area and identify rock types before mining - it shows what resources each rock contains.
Place your starting Burner Mining Drill on the iron ore patch. Place a Stone Furnace adjacent with its output facing the furnace input.
Fuel the drill with wood first (press Z with wood selected to quick-insert). Gather coal as fast as possible to replace wood fuel.
Craft enough Stone Furnaces from mined stone: aim for 8-10 iron furnaces and 4-6 copper furnaces. Use Shift + LClick to craft the max possible.
Place Burner Mining Drills on the iron patch feeding into your row of stone furnaces. Set up at least 8 furnaces smelting iron.
Build a coal "snake" - a chain of Burner Mining Drills on coal where each drill output feeds fuel into the next drill, making the coal patch self-fueling. Kick-start it with a few coal pieces.
Set up 4-6 copper furnaces with drills feeding them. Copper is needed for wire, circuits, and science packs.
While waiting for materials, hand-craft intermediate items: Iron Gear Wheels, Copper Cables - these will be used constantly. Use Shift + LClick to queue max crafting.
Don't destroy all trees - trees absorb pollution and keep biters calm in the early game. Chop selectively.
⚠
New Player Trap Many beginners spend too long in the full-manual phase. As soon as you have any coal production, start building drills and furnaces. Don't wait until you "have enough" - you never feel like you have enough until you automate.
💡
Pro Tip When you have excess coal, hold Ctrl + RClick on a machine to insert half a stack at once instead of repeatedly pressing Z. Great for bulk-fueling furnaces.
Phase 02
First Power & Red Science
Electricity, a lab, and your first technology
In Progress
▼
💡
Electricity unlocks everything. Without power, all your assemblers and research labs are paperweights. This is the bridge from the stone age to the automation age.
🏁
Phase Goal: Steam power running, lab built, Automation technology researched.
Power Setup
Craft: 1x Offshore Pump, 1x Boiler, 2x Steam Engine, several Pipes, and Small Electric Poles.
Place the Offshore Pump on the edge of a water body. Connect with pipes to the Boiler, then pipes from boiler to Steam Engines. Rotate buildings with R to align pipe connections.
Put coal in the boiler (or use an inserter feeding coal from a chest). The boiler turns water to steam, steam engines make electricity.
Drag Small Electric Poles from the steam engine toward your base to distribute power. Poles connect automatically if they are within reach of each other.
⚙
Key Ratio - Early Steam Power 1 Boiler : 2 Steam Engines
Each boiler feeds exactly 2 steam engines. Scale in multiples of this. A 5 boiler + 10 steam engine setup (900 kW) is a solid early power plant.
Lab & Research
Crafting 10 Copper Plates unlocks the Lab in your research tree (a trigger-based unlock in v2.x). Craft a Lab and place it near your base with power connected.
Hand-craft 10 Red Science Packs (Automation Science Packs). Recipe: 1 Copper Plate + 1 Iron Gear Wheel each.
Insert the 10 red science packs into the lab with Ctrl + LClick on an empty inventory slot - this auto-loads all matching items.
Queue the Automation technology in the research tree (T). Also queue Logistics (underground belts + splitters) - these are essential.
While waiting for research: craft Assembling Machines and plan where you'll put them. Also craft Iron Gear Wheels and Copper Cables in bulk.
⚠
New Player Trap The game doesn't pause while you're in menus or the research tree by default. Change this in settings if you want breathing room. Also: when electricity drops to zero and the "power gauge" goes red, check if your coal supply to the boiler ran out - it's almost always a fuel issue.
💡
Pro Tip Click on any item or building in the crafting menu with Alt + Click to open the Factoriopedia - it shows all recipes and production chains. Invaluable for figuring out what you need to build something.
Phase 03
Automation - Red & Green Science
Your first automated production lines
In Progress
▼
💡
This is where Factorio clicks. Assembling Machines handle crafting so you don't have to. Getting red and green science flowing automatically means your tech progresses while you work on other things - research never stops again.
🏁
Phase Goal: Automated red + green science flowing into at least 2 labs. Logistics tech researched.
Temporary Automation Setup
Craft several Assembling Machine 1s (from Automation research). These can craft most items automatically.
Set up 2 Assembling Machines making Iron Gear Wheels. Feed iron plates via Inserter from a Chest. Use Shift + RClick to copy a recipe, Shift + LClick to paste it.
Set up 1-2 Assembling Machines making Copper Cables. Output cables into a chest, then have inserters pull cables into the circuit assembler.
Set up 2 Assembling Machines making Green Circuits (Electronic Circuits). Recipe: 3 Copper Cable + 1 Iron Plate.
Set up Red Science (Automation Science Pack) production: 1 Copper Plate + 1 Iron Gear Wheel. Use 2 assemblers for this.
Run an output Inserter from the red science assembler into a chest, and another Inserter from that chest into the Lab. Or run a belt directly to the lab.
Once Logistics is researched, set up Green Science (Logistic Science Pack): 1 Transport Belt + 1 Inserter. Needs its own assembly line.
Add a second (and third) Lab and connect them to both science pack feeds. More labs = faster research.
Research queue in order: Logistics (underground belts), Fast Inserter, Steel Processing, Military (for turrets). Keep labs fed constantly.
Set up automated Transport Belt production - you will always need more belts. Belts need iron gears, so tap your gear output.
Set up automated Inserter production - you also always need more inserters.
💡
Pro Tip Think of Assembling Machines as 3x3 tiles. When placing them side by side, build one, then use its footprint as a spacer - you always know you have exactly 3 tiles between centers. This makes clean builds much easier.
⚠
New Player Trap - Production Deadlock If an assembler stops producing, check: (1) Is there power? (2) Are input chests empty? (3) Are output chests full (backed up)? A full output chest stalls the entire line. Always give output a place to go.
⚙
Key Ratio - Green Science Green science = 1 Transport Belt + 1 Inserter.
1 belt assembler + 1 gear assembler + 1 inserter assembler = ~1 green science assembler
You'll want at least 2-3 green science assemblers to keep labs fed.
Phase 04
Smelting & Bus Foundation
Building the backbone of your base
In Progress
▼
💡
A proper smelting array and a Main Bus layout are what separate functional bases from spaghetti nightmares. The main bus is the single most important structural decision for a new player - it makes expansion clean and logical.
🏁
Phase Goal: 16-20 iron furnaces, 8-10 copper furnaces, stone bricks flowing, main bus started.
Proper Smelting Columns
Build an Iron Smelting Column: a straight line of 16-20 Stone Furnaces. Belt ore in on one side, belt plates out the other side. Inserters load/unload each furnace.
Build a Copper Smelting Column: 8-10 Stone Furnaces in the same arrangement. Copper is less needed early but scales up significantly mid-game.
Build a Stone Smelting Column: 4-6 furnaces making Stone Bricks. Stone bricks are used for concrete (later) and are also a raw bus material.
Make sure coal miners are producing enough coal to fuel all furnaces AND your boilers. If furnaces go dark, you need more coal miners.
⚙
Key Ratio - Stone Furnace Smelting 1 Burner Mining Drill : ~1 Stone Furnace (rough match)
A full yellow belt (15/sec) needs about 13-14 stone furnaces to keep pace. Build 16-20 for buffer.
The Main Bus
Pick a direction from your smelters and lay down a straight row of belts - this is your Main Bus. Production feeds into the bus; everything else branches off it.
Use groups of 4 belts with 2 empty tiles between groups. The 2-tile gap allows underground belts to cross underneath - critical for routing items off the bus.
Recommended bus lanes (left to right): Iron Plates (4 lanes), Copper Plates (2-4 lanes), Steel (1-2 lanes), Green Circuits (2-4 lanes), Stone Bricks (1 lane). You won't fill them all immediately.
To pull items off the bus: clear a gap, place a Splitter, use underground belts to route the split lane to your factory area. Underground belts can pass under the bus without interrupting it.
Leave way more space than you think you need between production areas. Factories always expand - cramped designs become painful fast.
Move your Science Pack production to pull from the bus instead of dedicated chests - this is cleaner and easier to scale.
⚠
New Player Trap - Spaghetti Thinking The temptation is to connect every machine to every other machine directly. Resist. The Main Bus forces you to think in terms of a shared resource highway. It feels limiting at first but makes every future addition trivial - just tap the bus.
💡
Pro Tip Don't build the whole bus at once. Put down lane markers (ghost belts or just physical belts) in the right positions now, and fill them in as smelting capacity grows. This reserves the space and gives you a layout to build toward.
Phase 05
Military Science
Defense, turrets, and fighting back
In Progress
▼
💡
Biters attack your base periodically - and attacks get bigger over time as your pollution spreads. Military science unlocks better weapons, walls, and turrets that keep your factory safe while you focus on building.
🏁
Phase Goal: Defended perimeter with gun turrets + walls, automated ammo production, military science flowing.
Defense Setup
Research Military tech (requires red + green science). This unlocks Gun Turrets, Walls, Grenades upgrades.
Build stone Walls around the perimeter of your base. Walls stop biters from walking through - they bite at walls instead. Build walls at least 2-3 tiles thick in high-traffic areas.
Place Gun Turrets behind the walls every 5-7 tiles. Turrets fire automatically at biters. Each turret needs Firearm Magazines (ammo) inserted manually or via inserter from a chest.
Set up automated Iron Ammo (Firearm Magazines) production feeding into chests near turrets. Recipe: 4 Iron Plates = 1 magazine.
Actively push back biter nests near your base. Move left-right while shooting to dodge their projectiles. Bring fish (healing) from nearby water if available.
Military Science Packs
Military Science Pack recipe: 1 Firearm Magazine + 1 Grenade + 2 Wall. Set up assemblers pulling from the bus for iron and stone.
Automate Grenade production: 5 Coal + 10 Iron Plate. Coal should be on your bus or at least accessible from a chest.
Feed military science into your labs alongside red and green. Research priorities: Turret Damage upgrades, Physical Projectile Damage, Steel Processing if not yet done.
⚠
New Player Trap - Neglecting Defense A biter breach that destroys your power poles or assemblers can set you back hours. Build walls and turrets proactively, before you feel like you need them. A defended base lets you build in peace.
💡
Pro Tip When fighting biters manually, strafe side-to-side while shooting. Biter projectiles are slow and easy to dodge with movement. Grenades are extremely effective against groups - throw one into a cluster before shooting.
Phase 06
Oil & Blue Science
The biggest complexity jump in the game
In Progress
▼
💡
Oil introduces fluids - a completely different logistics system from belts. It's where most new players get stuck. But once you have blue science flowing, the mid-game opens up completely and progression accelerates dramatically.
🏁
Phase Goal: Oil refinery running, plastic + sulfur production, red circuits automated, blue science flowing into labs.
Oil Setup
Research Oil Processing (requires blue science beakers to unlock advanced processing later, but Basic Oil Processing is available from red+green+military science).
Find an Oil Patch on the map. Build an Oil Refinery on or adjacent to oil wells (connect oil wells to refinery with pipes).
Set refinery to Basic Oil Processing initially. This produces: Petroleum Gas (most useful early), Light Oil, Heavy Oil.
Add Pumps in pipe networks longer than ~20 pipes. In v2.x, long pipes without pumps lose pressure and stop flowing. When a pipe network stops working, add a pump.
Build a Chemical Plant for Plastic production. Recipe: 1 Coal + 2 Petroleum Gas = 2 Plastic Bars. Coal from the bus, gas from a pipe.
Build a Chemical Plant for Sulfur. Recipe: 2 Water + 3 Petroleum Gas = 2 Sulfur. Sulfur is needed for blue science and sulfuric acid.
Add plastic and sulfur to your main bus (1-2 lanes each).
Red Circuits & Blue Science
Set up Advanced Circuit (Red Circuit) production. Recipe: 4 Copper Cable + 2 Green Circuit + 2 Plastic Bar. These are slow to make - build several assemblers.
Add Red Circuits to your main bus (2 lanes). They're used everywhere in mid and late game.
Set up Engine Unit production for blue science. Recipe: 1 Steel Pipe + 8 Iron Gear Wheel + 1 Steel Plate (x1 crafted in assembler). Also needs Chemical Plant for some variants - check the recipe.
Set up Blue Science (Chemical Science Pack). Recipe: 3 Advanced Circuit + 2 Engine Unit + 1 Sulfur. Hook assemblers to the bus.
Feed blue science into all labs. Research priorities: Advanced Oil Processing, Robotics (critically important), Construction Robotics.
Switch refinery to Advanced Oil Processing once researched. This adds water input and produces more useful outputs. Also research and use Coal Liquefaction or Cracking to balance oil product ratios.
⚠
New Player Trap - Oil Backup If any oil product (petroleum gas, light oil, heavy oil) completely fills its pipe/storage, the refinery STOPS. You must always have consumers for all three products or use cracking to convert excess into what you need. Heavy oil -> Light oil cracking and Light oil -> Petroleum gas cracking fix this.
⚠
New Player Trap - Red Circuits Are Slow Advanced Circuits are the slowest to produce of anything you've made so far. Don't be surprised when your blue science factory crawls because red circuits can't keep up. You need 4-8 assemblers dedicated just to red circuits. Scale this up early.
⚙
Key Ratio - Advanced Oil Processing Advanced Oil Processing: 100 crude + 50 water = 25 heavy + 45 light + 55 petroleum gas
For balancing: use Heavy Oil Cracking (40 heavy + 30 water = 30 light oil) and Light Oil Cracking (30 light + 30 water = 20 petroleum gas) to turn excess into petroleum gas.
Phase 07
Construction Robots
The game-changer that transforms building
In Progress
▼
💡
Construction robots are Factorio's defining feature. Once you have robots, you stop manually placing every building and instead blueprint and let bots do the work. This enables exponential scaling - you design, robots execute.
🏁
Phase Goal: Roboports deployed, construction bots active, personal roboport equipped, logistic network running.
Robot Rush Build
Research path: Robotics (blue science) -> Construction Robotics -> Logistic Robotics. Make this your top research priority after blue science flows.
Build Flying Robot Frame production. Recipe requires Electric Engine Units which need Lubricant (from Light Oil in Chemical Plant) and Electric Engine parts.
Produce Construction Bots: Flying Robot Frame + Green Circuit + Iron Gear Wheel. Build at least 50, ideally 100+ before expanding heavily.
Build Roboports (4x4 buildings). Each roboport covers a charging and logistics area. Place them so their coverage (grey circle) overlaps - no dead zones.
Load construction bots into roboports. They'll now automatically deconstruct marked items and build ghost blueprints within range.
Research Personal Roboport Equipment. Build it and insert into your armor grid. Now you carry a personal roboport - your own bots fly out from your character to build things.
Build Logistic Bots and place them in roboports. Logistic bots move items between provider chests and requester chests - the foundation of the logistic network.
Keep producing more bots - 50 is a start, 200+ is where things get genuinely fast. The more bots you have, the faster everything gets built.
💡
Pro Tip - The Robot Rush Many experienced players prioritize getting robots as fast as possible, even accepting a slower science rate during the rush. Robots pay back the investment immediately - you stop spending time manually placing every belt and inserter. It's worth temporarily straining your base to get here quickly.
⚠
New Player Trap - Too Few Bots With only 20-30 bots you'll see them constantly busy but work barely progresses. The impact is minimal. At 100+ bots, things happen noticeably faster. At 200-500, it feels like magic. Always be producing more bots.
⚠
New Player Trap - Power for Charging Robots need to charge at roboports. With many bots flying around, charging demand spikes. If your power is weak, bots will queue at chargers and slow down. Getting solar power (Phase 8) goes hand-in-hand with scaling up robots.
Phase 08
Solar Power
Stable, scalable, pollution-free power
In Progress
▼
💡
Coal power works but it requires constant coal supply, produces pollution that aggravates biters, and doesn't scale cleanly. Solar + accumulators are zero-maintenance once placed and robots can build huge arrays quickly.
🏁
Phase Goal: Large solar array supplementing or replacing coal power, laser turret defense upgrade, power stable for expansion.
Solar Array Setup
Research Solar Energy (blue science) and Electric Energy Accumulator.
Set up Battery production: Chemical Plant, Sulfuric Acid + Iron Plate + Copper Plate. Batteries are needed for Accumulators.
Produce Solar Panels and Accumulators in bulk - you'll need hundreds to thousands eventually. Robots will place them.
Blueprint a solar tile pattern and have robots build it. Use the 25:21 ratio - every group of 25 solar panels needs 21 accumulators.
Include roboports in your solar blueprint so bots can reach and build the furthest panels.
Keep coal boilers as backup power initially. Watch the power production graph - if accumulators are always depleting overnight, add more solar panels and accumulators.
⚙
Key Ratio - Solar Power 25 Solar Panels : 21 Accumulators
This ratio ensures accumulators fill during the day and are fully drained by morning - perfect efficiency. Each solar panel produces 60 kW peak. Each accumulator stores 5 MJ.
Laser Turret Upgrade
Research Laser Turrets. These use electricity instead of ammo - no more manual ammo resupply at turrets.
Build Laser Turrets using the battery production you already have (laser turrets need batteries in their recipe). Replace gun turrets at the perimeter.
Note: laser turrets draw a lot of power during biter attacks. Make sure your power grid can handle spikes - this is another reason to have robust solar + accumulators.
💡
Pro Tip - Solar Design Pattern Avoid rotationally symmetric solar layouts - they look clean but create dead zones. Instead use a rectangular repeating tile that can be stamped side-by-side and top-to-bottom. Design once, stamp everywhere with robots.
Phase 09
Yellow Science (Production Science)
Do this BEFORE purple science
In Progress
▼
💡
Yellow science unlocks Personal Roboport Mk2, Power Armor Mk2, Battery Mk2, and the full logistics system. These make you vastly more effective as a builder. Despite appearing "after" purple in the UI, do yellow first - the quality-of-life upgrades are worth far more at this stage.
🏁
Phase Goal: Yellow science flowing, red belts deployed, steel furnaces upgraded, personal roboport equipped.
Base Upgrades First
Use robots to upgrade all yellow belts to Red Belts (Fast Transport Belts). Build up a stockpile of red belts first, then stamp the upgrade - robots do all the work.
Upgrade all Stone Furnaces to Steel Furnaces. Same footprint, double the smelting speed. This doubles your throughput without building more furnaces.
Scale up Green Circuit production significantly - blue circuits are extremely green-circuit-hungry and you'll need far more than you currently make.
Yellow Science Ingredients
Set up Processing Unit (Blue Circuit) production. Recipe: 20 Green Circuit + 2 Advanced Circuit + 1 Sulfuric Acid. Needs Assembling Machine 2 (handles fluids). Very green-circuit-intensive - expect to build many parallel assemblers.
Set up Low Density Structure production. Recipe: 20 Copper Plate + 5 Steel + 2 Plastic Bar. Copper-heavy - expand your copper smelting column.
Set up Flying Robot Frame production (needed for yellow science pack AND for making more bots). Recipe: 1 Steel + 2 Battery + 3 Electronic Circuit + 1 Electric Engine Unit.
Set up Yellow Science (Production Science Pack) production. Recipe: 1 Electric Engine Unit + 1 Low Density Structure + 1 Processing Unit + 1 Rail.
Add Blue Circuits and Low Density Structures to your main bus - they are late-game staples.
Research priority with yellow science: Power Armor Mk2, Personal Roboport Mk2, Battery Mk2, Energy Shield Mk2, Logistics System.
⚠
New Player Trap - Blue Circuit Bottleneck Processing Units (blue circuits) are the most resource-intensive item you've crafted so far. A single assembler makes about 1 per 10 seconds. You'll need 6-10 assemblers minimum to keep yellow science and future rocket production supplied. Plan for a large blue circuit factory from the start.
⚙
Key Ratio - Blue Circuit Production 1 blue circuit assembler needs ~10 green circuit assemblers to keep it fed
Blue circuits consume 20 green circuits each. At 1 blue/10 seconds, that's 2 green circuits/sec - roughly what 10 green circuit assemblers produce.
Phase 10
Power Armor & Logistics Network
You become a construction god
In Progress
▼
💡
Power Armor with personal roboports transforms your character into a mobile construction machine. Combined with the logistic network (requester/provider chests), you can deploy entire factories from your inventory with bots doing all the physical placement.
🏁
Phase Goal: Power Armor Mk2 equipped with portable solar + Battery Mk2 + personal roboports + shields, logistic network active.
Craft Power Armor Mk2. This has a large equipment grid for modules. Recipe: expensive - needs processing units and low density structures.
Power the armor grid with Portable Solar Panels plus Battery Mk2 equipment. Solar charges the batteries by day, the batteries carry you through the night and cover burst draw from roboports and shields.
Install Personal Roboport Mk2 equipment. Multiple roboports = larger build radius and more simultaneous bot slots.
Install Energy Shield Mk2 equipment for combat protection when clearing biter nests.
Install Exoskeleton equipment for faster movement speed.
Set up the Logistic Network: place Provider Chests (items go in) and Requester Chests (items come out, you set what you want). Logistic bots handle the transport automatically within roboport coverage.
Set a Logistic Request on yourself (character inventory) for common items: belts, inserters, poles, etc. Bots will automatically keep your inventory stocked from the network.
Use Active Provider Chests in production lines (bots proactively pull from these) and Passive Provider Chests for storage (bots pull only when something requests it).
💡
Pro Tip - Equipment Balance Fill remaining armor slots with exoskeletons for movement speed - it makes a huge quality-of-life difference. A workable Mk2 loadout: 2 personal roboports Mk2, 2 energy shields Mk2, 4-6 portable solar panels, 2-3 Battery Mk2, exoskeletons filling the rest.
⚠
No Portable Fusion Reactor in the base game The Portable Fusion Reactor is Space Age DLC content - it does not exist in vanilla 2.0. Your armor grid runs on portable solar and batteries only, which means power is genuinely tight: roboports, shields and exoskeletons all draw at once. Budget grid space for power the way you would budget a real factory, and expect to stand still occasionally to recharge.
Phase 11
Nuclear Power
Abundant power for the endgame
In Progress
▼
💡
Purple science (Phase 12) unlocks beacons and Tier 3 modules which massively increase power consumption. Nuclear power provides the abundant, reliable megawatts needed for a fully beaconed endgame factory. Build nuclear before purple science, not after.
🏁
Phase Goal: Uranium mining active, at least 4-reactor nuclear plant running, 500+ MW available.
Uranium Setup
Research Uranium Mining and Nuclear Power (yellow + blue science). Also research Atomic Bomb branch leads to Kovarex enrichment later.
Find Uranium ore on the map. Uranium mining requires Sulfuric Acid piped to the miners (unique requirement - normal drills need fluid input).
Set up Sulfuric Acid production if not already done (Chemical Plant: Sulfur + Iron Plate + Water). Pipe it to the uranium mine.
Use a train to bring uranium ore back to base (or run pipes for sulfuric acid out and belt uranium back). A single 1-4 train (1 tank car for sulfuric acid, 3-4 cargo cars for uranium) works perfectly.
Build Uranium Processing in Centrifuges. This produces U-235 (~0.7%) and U-238 (~99.3%). U-235 is nuclear fuel. U-238 is mostly waste (save 40 U-235 for Kovarex later).
Build Nuclear Fuel Cell production: 10 U-235 + 1 Iron Plate = 10 fuel cells. A single centrifuge output is more than enough for 4-8 reactors.
Reactor Plant
Place reactors close to a large water source. This is more important than being near uranium. Heat exchangers need enormous amounts of water.
Build a 4-reactor minimum setup (2x2 arrangement). Reactors touching each other get a neighbor bonus - each extra neighbor doubles output. A 4-reactor array outputs 480 MW.
Chain: Reactor (heat) -> Heat Pipes -> Heat Exchangers -> Steam -> Steam Turbines -> Power. Each reactor needs ~heat exchangers and turbines downstream.
Use the logistic network to supply fuel cells to reactors and remove spent fuel cells automatically.
Store 40+ U-235 in reserve for Kovarex Enrichment (optional tech that makes more U-235 from U-238 - great for late game but not required for rocket).
⚙
Key Ratio - Nuclear Power Per reactor in a 2x2 array (120 MW effective each):
1 Reactor : 12 Heat Exchangers : 21 Steam Turbines
So the full 4-reactor array (480 MW) needs ~48 heat exchangers and ~83 turbines. Place near water, not near uranium - heat exchangers drink enormous amounts of it.
⚠
New Player Trap - Kovarex Is Optional You do NOT need Kovarex enrichment to run a nuclear plant. Basic uranium processing with a single centrifuge produces plenty of U-235 for any reasonably sized plant. Don't delay nuclear because you don't have Kovarex yet.
Phase 12
Purple Science (Utility Science)
The final tech tier before rockets
In Progress
▼
💡
Purple science is surprisingly straightforward compared to yellow - it only needs steel and red circuits. But it unlocks Tier 3 assemblers, Tier 3 modules, beacons, and blue belts which are all critical for rocket production throughput.
🏁
Phase Goal: Purple science flowing, Tier 3 modules unlocked, beacons researched, Tier 3 assemblers available.
Scale up Red Circuit (Advanced Circuit) production significantly before starting purple science. You'll need a full bus lane or more.
Scale up Steel production - purple science needs a lot. Upgrade to steel furnaces (from Phase 9) and add more smelting columns if needed.
Set up Purple Science (Utility Science Pack) production. Recipe: 3 Advanced Circuit + 1 Flying Robot Frame + 2 Low Density Structure.
Feed purple science into labs alongside all other science types. Research priorities: Productivity Module 3, Speed Module 3, Beacon, Tier 3 Assembling Machine.
Research Kovarex Enrichment if you want more U-235 (optional but nice for late game).
Research Blue Belt (Express Transport Belt) - extremely expensive to make but gives highest throughput. Not strictly necessary for first rocket but helps with late game.
💡
Pro Tip - Research Order Prioritize Productivity Module 3 before anything else from purple science. A Productivity 3 module in your Rocket Silo reduces rocket parts needed from 1000 to ~715 each - a massive saving. Get Tier 3 assemblers second for the 4-module slots (needed for proper beacon setups).
Phase 13
Modules & Beacons
Massive throughput multipliers
In Progress
▼
💡
Productivity modules reduce the resources needed per output. Speed beacons multiply production speed of everything in range. Combined, they transform your factory from barely sufficient to massively overproducing - this is what makes a rocket launch achievable without building an enormous factory.
🏁
Phase Goal: Productivity 3 modules in key buildings, speed beacons deployed, oil fully beaconed and upgraded.
Build Productivity Module 3 production. Very expensive (blue circuits + red circuits + green circuits). Stockpile 50-100 before deploying.
Build Speed Module 3 production. Equally expensive. You'll need many for beacons.
Build Beacons. Each beacon transmits its module effects to all machines within 3 tiles. Speed Module 3 beacons surrounding an assembler give massive speed boosts.
Install Productivity Module 3 in: Rocket Silo (4 slots - this is the highest priority), all Blue Circuit assemblers, all Green Circuit assemblers, Oil Refinery.
Surround key production buildings with Speed Beacons (each containing 2x Speed Module 3). Buildings in range of 4+ beacons get enormous speed multipliers.
Upgrade Oil Refinery with Productivity 3 modules + surrounding speed beacons. This dramatically increases oil output from the same inputs.
Add Tier 3 (yellow) Assembling Machines where possible - they hold 4 module slots vs 2 for Tier 2. Use them for the most bottlenecked production steps.
Upgrade Nuclear Fuel Cell production with Productivity 3 modules in a Tier 3 assembler - you get more fuel from the same U-235.
⚠
New Player Trap - Module Power Drain Speed modules dramatically increase power consumption of the machines they're in. Productivity modules in beacons are banned (only speed/efficiency in beacons). Before deploying beacons everywhere, make sure your nuclear plant is online and producing abundantly. A power sag with beacons deployed will tank your entire factory.

Key Ratio - Rocket Silo with Prod 3 1000 rocket parts needed -> ~715 with 4x Productivity Module 3 in silo
This saves ~285 of each rocket part. At rocket scale, that's a substantial reduction. Always fill the silo with Productivity 3.

The final production push

A rocket is 100 Rocket Parts, and each part costs 10 Processing Units + 10 Low Density Structures + 10 Rocket Fuel. Three components, produced in quantity. Each one is expensive, and the silo is slow to fill. This phase is about scaling every upstream production line enough to feed the silo at a meaningful rate while optionally building a satellite for the complete ending.
🏁
Phase Goal: Rocket Silo placed with Prod 3 modules, all 3 rocket components flowing in, satellite crafted and ready.
Rocket Silo
Research Rocket Silo. Craft it (very expensive - needs blue circuits, steel, low density structures). Place it with plenty of space around it for inserters and belts.
Insert 4x Productivity Module 3 into the Rocket Silo. This is mandatory - don't launch without them.
The Three Rocket Components
Rocket Fuel
  • 10 Solid Fuel + 10 Light Oil
  • Solid Fuel: 10 Light Oil or 20 Petroleum Gas
  • Scale up oil production heavily
  • Needs: lots of light oil -> solid fuel -> rocket fuel
Processing Unit (blue circuit)
  • 20 Green Circuits + 2 Red Circuits + 5 Sulfuric Acid
  • Massive strain on green circuit production
  • Add many parallel assemblers
Low Density Structure
  • 20 Copper + 2 Steel + 5 Plastic
  • Double existing production (needed for both yellow science and rockets)
  • Copper-intensive - expand copper smelting
Satellite (Optional)
  • 100 Low Density Structure
  • 100 Solar Panel
  • 100 Accumulator
  • 100 Processing Unit
  • 50 Rocket Fuel
  • 5 Radar
  • Craft 1 manually or in assembler
Set up Rocket Fuel production: Light Oil -> Solid Fuel (Chemical Plant) -> Rocket Fuel (Chemical Plant). Scale oil refinery output with beacons and modules first.
Scale up Processing Unit production. Add dedicated assemblers - this is the hardest of the three to sustain, so run many parallel blue circuit assemblers fed by their own green and red circuit lines.
Double your Low Density Structure production - rockets need as much as yellow science does.
Scale up Green Circuits - ideally fill an entire city block with green circuit production to feed blue circuits at scale.
Belt or bot-deliver all three rocket components into the Rocket Silo via inserters.
Craft a Satellite. You can do this manually if you have the materials - it only needs one. Insert it into the rocket silo via inserter or manually.
Watch the silo fill up. A rocket is 100 parts, so 1000 of each component - with 4x Productivity 3 modules that drops to ~715 of each. Track progress in the silo UI.
⚠
New Player Trap - Underestimating Scale Every production line you've built so far will feel inadequate when feeding a rocket silo. The silo needs sustained production of all three components simultaneously. Check which component fills slowest and expand that production first. The bottleneck shifts as you fix each one.
💡
Pro Tip - Satellite Worth It Launching with a satellite completes the game fully and gives you permanent satellite view over the entire map. It takes extra resources but is very achievable. Stock solar panels and accumulators from your Phase 8 production. The satellite uses 100 of each - just pull from stockpiles.
